Free running and strength calculators

The same maths that runs inside the Forge app, free in your browser. Nothing you type here leaves your device.

  • ⏱Running pace calculatorPace, finish time or distance from any two of the three, with splits.
  • 👟Training paces calculatorEasy, steady, threshold and race-pace training paces from a recent race.
  • 🏁Race time predictorWhat one race result suggests for every other distance, with a likely range.
  • 🏋One-rep max calculatorEstimate your 1RM from a hard set, and the weight for 1 to 12 reps.
  • 📏Body fat calculatorEstimate body fat from a tape measure, with a likely range.
  • 🔥Maintenance calorie calculatorDaily calorie burn from your size, activity and training, plus a protein range.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type here is sent anywhere or saved. They give estimates for training, not a promise.

Why they are free

Forge is an app that plans lifting and running together. These are the calculators inside it, put on the web so anyone can use them without signing up. Each page says where its method comes from and what it cannot tell you.

The next step

Forge Strength and Forge Run turn them into a plan: the week for your gym or hotel room, and the runs that build toward your race, with weights and paces that rise when you have earned them.
