How it works

Forge estimates body fat several ways from the same tape measure and your height, weight and age, puts each on one scale (the one a DXA scan reads), and blends them, giving more weight to the ones that are usually closer.

Men get four estimates: a waist and weight equation fitted to DXA scans, the US Navy circumference method, relative fat mass (from your height and waist), and one from your BMI and age. Women get the same, without the Navy method, because it needs the narrowest part of the waist and this page asks for the waist at the navel.

Lean mass is your weight less the fat mass. The table shows what each method says by itself, so you can see how far apart they are.

Where the method comes from

  • Taylor KM, et al. (2024). One-site Army waist equations referenced to DXA, with the size of each method's offset. doi:10.1002/osp4.747.
  • Hodgdon JA, Beckett MB (1984). The US Navy circumference method for body fat from the neck, waist and height.
  • Woolcott OO, Bergman RN (2018). Relative fat mass (RFM) as a new estimator of whole-body fat percentage. Scientific Reports 8:10980. doi:10.1038/s41598-018-29362-1.
  • Deurenberg P, Weststrate JA, Seidell JC (1991). Body mass index as a measure of body fatness: age- and sex-specific prediction formulas. British Journal of Nutrition 65(2):105-114. doi:10.1079/bjn19910073.
  • Foulis SA, et al. (2023). Navy-type circumference equations read lower than DXA in young recruits. Frontiers in Physiology 14:1183836. doi:10.3389/fphys.2023.1183836.

What it cannot tell you

It is an estimate based on published research and what you enter. Tape methods are typically within about 3.5 points of a DXA scan, and the likely range shows that; about twice that is possible.

Very lean or very muscular people, and people who carry weight in an unusual pattern, can sit well outside it. Tape and weight formulas cannot tell muscle from fat.

It is for training and tracking, not a health check or a medical test. The same tape, the same spot and the same time of day matter more than any one reading: watch the trend.
