How it works

Forge works your resting burn out from your lean mass, which it takes from the body fat you enter or estimates from your height, weight and age. On top of that it adds the burn of an ordinary day (more if you are on your feet), and the extra burn from the training you list.

Maintenance is what you would eat to hold your weight steady. A modest amount below it lets weight drift down slowly, a modest amount above it lets it drift up. Forge sets the exact figure in the app from your own numbers.

The protein range runs from 0.73 grams per pound of bodyweight (1.6 g per kg), where extra protein stops adding much for most people who lift, to 1.0 g per pound (2.2 g per kg), the top of the range that review found. Lean toward the top if you lift hard, or when you are eating less. If you are well above a weight you would be happy at, base it on a weight nearer that.

Where the method comes from

  • Cunningham JJ (1991). Body composition as a determinant of energy expenditure: a synthetic review and a proposed general prediction equation. American Journal of Clinical Nutrition 54:963-969. doi:10.1093/ajcn/54.6.963. Resting burn from lean mass.
  • Tinsley GM, Graybeal AJ, Moore ML (2018). Resting metabolic rate in muscular physique athletes: validity of existing methods and development of new prediction equations. Applied Physiology, Nutrition, and Metabolism 44:397-406. doi:10.1139/apnm-2018-0412. Why the resting range is shown.
  • Westerterp KR (2004). Diet induced thermogenesis. Nutrition & Metabolism 1:5. doi:10.1186/1743-7075-1-5. The cost of digesting food is inside the activity factor.
  • Institute of Medicine (2005). Dietary Reference Intakes for Energy. The physical activity levels the three daily-activity choices follow.
  • Morton RW, et al. (2018). A systematic review, meta-analysis and meta-regression of the effect of protein supplementation on resistance training-induced gains in muscle mass and strength. British Journal of Sports Medicine 52:376-384. doi:10.1136/bjsports-2017-097608. The protein range.
  • Deurenberg P, Weststrate JA, Seidell JC (1991). British Journal of Nutrition 65(2):105-114. doi:10.1079/bjn19910073. The body fat estimate when you do not enter one.

What it cannot tell you

It is an estimate. Published equations disagree by a few hundred calories, and people with a lot of muscle often burn more than they predict. The resting range shows that spread; the real number is yours to find.

The best check is your weight over two to three weeks at a steady intake: if it is flat, you are eating about maintenance. Forge does this for you once you log food and weight.

It is for training and general fitness, not medical advice and not a weight-loss plan. If you have a health condition, are pregnant, or are under 18, talk to your doctor or dietitian first.
