Getting started

How does Forge pick a workout?

On Today, choose how many minutes you have and, if you like, which muscles to train. Forge looks at the equipment at your current place, how many hard sets each muscle has had this week against its target, and how recovered each muscle is, and builds a session from that. A first run opens on a short setup with a coach who asks a few questions and makes the rest from your answers.

Can I change the workout it builds?

Yes. Before you start you can swap or add exercises, add cardio, or save it for later. During a workout you can add or remove exercises and sets.

Do I need a gym?

No. Set up a place with only bodyweight, or a few dumbbells, and Forge builds from that. Tap the place button at the top of the screen to add a place, change its equipment, or switch to where you are.

How does Forge decide my weights and reps?

Forge uses double progression: it shows what you did last time, and when you reach the top of the rep range on every set it suggests a little more weight next time. It goes by what you actually lifted, not a fixed weekly increase.

Something hurts. What should I do?

Tap Something hurting? on Today or the Body tab and choose the area. Forge leaves out the moves that load it until you tap Feels better. Forge cannot tell what is wrong. If pain is sharp, comes with swelling or numbness, or is there at rest, stop and get it checked.

TestFlight (the beta)

How do I join the beta?

Install Apple's TestFlight app, then open the invitation link from our email or from forgetrainanywhere.com/beta. Tapping Install or Update in TestFlight installs Forge. Beta builds expire after 90 days; install the newest build from TestFlight to keep going.

How do I send feedback?

Use Send feedback or Report a bug inside Forge, or write to support@forgetrainanywhere.com. In TestFlight you can also take a screenshot and share it with the feedback sheet. We read all of it.

Will I lose my data when the beta becomes the App Store version?

Install the App Store version over the TestFlight one with the same Apple ID and your data stays. If you use a Forge account with cloud sync, signing in on any phone brings it back. To be safe, download a backup first (see Your data and privacy).

Forge Strength and subscriptions

What is free and what is paid?

Forge is free to download. Logging workouts, the exercise library, tape measurements and the weight log, your character in a still pose, and backup and restore are free. Forge Strength adds planning ahead, long-range projections, the nutrition lab, more focus areas, workout styles and places, motion and sharing options, Send to Garmin for lifting workouts, and cloud sync. Open More → Forge Strength in the app to see exactly what it adds.

What does it cost?

$9.99 a month, or $59.99 a year with a 2-week free trial for new subscribers. Apple shows the exact price for your country before you confirm. Payment is charged to your Apple Account, and the subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Open your iPhone's Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Forge, or tap Manage subscription on the Forge Strength screen in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. You keep Forge Strength until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the Forge app or your Forge account does not cancel a subscription.

I bought it but it isn't unlocked, or I have a new phone

Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought it with, then tap Restore Purchases on the Forge Strength screen (More → Forge Strength). Restore Purchases is also under the plans on the paywall. If you use a Forge account, signing in to it on the new phone brings the plan back too.

What happens to my data if I stop paying?

Nothing you saved is taken away. Places, focus areas and styles you already have keep working; Forge stops adding Strength-only options, and cloud sync pauses. Your data stays on your phone.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I use an offer code?

On the Forge Strength screen tap Redeem a code. Apple's code screen finishes the redemption.

Your data and privacy

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. Forge works fully offline and does not need an account. Your data leaves the phone only if you turn on cloud sync, connect a service such as Garmin, or export a backup. Our privacy policy lists everything, and our data retention policy says how long each kind of data is kept.

Can Forge read my synced data?

No. Synced workouts, measurements and settings are encrypted on your phone with a key made from your password before they are uploaded. We cannot read them and we cannot unlock them for you.

How do I back up my data or move to a new phone?

Go to Settings → Devices and data → Backup and restore and download the file; save it somewhere safe, such as Files or iCloud Drive. To restore, choose the file in the same place. Or sign in to your Forge account on the new phone if you use cloud sync.

How do I erase everything on this phone?

Go to Settings → About → More options → Erase all data on this device and type ERASE. Deleting the Forge app also removes everything it stored on that phone.

What does Forge do with Apple Health?

If you connect it (Settings → Devices and data → Apple Health), Forge reads your workouts, weight, body fat, steps and active energy, and saves the workouts you finish in Forge. It is used only for your own history, body log and estimates; it is never used for ads or marketing and never shared. You can disconnect it in Settings or in Apple's Health app.

Is there an age limit?

Forge can be used from age 11. Under 13, everything stays on the device: no account, no cloud, no analytics. A Forge account needs you to be 13 or older.

How do I stop Forge emails?

Use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any Forge email, or go to Settings → Account and choose Unsubscribe. We only send tips and news if you ticked the box.

Accounts and sync

Do I need an account?

No. An account is only for syncing between your devices and keeping an encrypted copy in the cloud, which is part of Forge Strength.

How do I turn on sync?

Go to Settings → Account → Sign in or create account. If the phone and the account both already have data, Forge asks whether to merge them (recommended), upload this phone's data, or replace it with the account's.

What is the recovery code?

A code Forge shows once when you set up sync. Keep it somewhere safe, such as a password manager. If you forget your password, it is the only way to unlock your synced data, because we cannot read or reset it.

I forgot my password

Tap Forgot password?, open the link we email you and set a new password, then enter your recovery code. No code? You can start over with the data on your phone; anything that existed only in the cloud is lost.

Sync says I'm signed out

Your login expired or was changed on another device. Your data is still on the phone; sign in again and sync picks up where it left off.

Deleting your account

You can delete your Forge account without writing to us:

  1. Go to Settings → Account and tap your signed-in account.
  2. Tap Delete cloud account. If you have an Apple subscription, Forge reminds you that deleting does not cancel it and links to Manage Subscriptions.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

Your login and everything synced to it are deleted from our live database right away. Forge then asks whether to keep a copy on this phone. If you cannot get into the app, email support@forgetrainanywhere.com from the address on the account and we will delete it for you.

Garmin

How do I send a workout to my Garmin watch?

You need a Forge account, Forge Strength for lifting workouts, and a Garmin watch that supports Connect IQ apps. Install the Forge app on your watch from Garmin's Connect IQ Store, open it (it shows a 6-letter code), then go to Settings → Devices and data → Garmin in Forge and type the code to link the watch. After that, Send to watch appears on a planned workout.

What does Forge send to Garmin?

Only the workout you choose to send: its name, exercises, sets, reps, rest and weights. It is held until your watch downloads it, at most 24 hours, and then erased. Unlink a watch or turn Send to watch off in the same place at any time.

Health and safety

Forge is a fitness and wellness app. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Body-fat figures, calorie targets and projections are estimates based on published research and what you enter; your results will differ, and a projection is not a promise. Check with a doctor before starting a new exercise or nutrition programme, especially if you have a health condition or an injury, or are pregnant. Stop if something hurts.
