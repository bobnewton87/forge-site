New to running?

Base first, run-walk to start

Tell Forge you're new and it starts with easy running and run-walk intervals, and builds a base before any hard sessions. If you race, it works backward from the race date, and it never promises a time.

  • Easy running and run-walk intervals first
  • A base built toward about five hours of easy running a week
  • Serious progressions when you're ready: tempo built up in stages, steady runs with a threshold finish, fartlek

Forge (Strength + Run)

Lifting that fits around your running

Forge plans your lifting and your running in the same week, so a hard run doesn't land the day before heavy legs, and your strength work supports the race you're training for.

  • One schedule: lift days, run days and rest days, with the minutes you have
  • Report an injury and Forge swaps the runs it rules out
  • Change your usual week and re-plan the weeks ahead in one tap

On your wrist

Send any workout to your Garmin

Pick a planned run or lift and send it to your Garmin watch. It needs a Garmin watch and a Forge sign-in, and you choose when to turn it on.

  • Runs with pace targets, strength days with their moves
  • One tap from the plan, or from the day's sheet

Once a runner, always a runner

Live the runner's dream

Start as a high-school freshman and race your way to the front. Train with your Forge Run plan or simulate the week, then race cross-country and the track against full fields. Your runner gets only as fast as the training you put in.

FAQ

Questions

Is Forge Run for beginners?

Yes. Tell Forge you're new and it starts with easy running and run-walk intervals, and builds a base before any hard sessions. If you race, it works backward from the race date. It never promises a time: it shows a likely range and what would change it.

Which events does Run cover?

Twelve: the 60 m, 100 m, 200 m, 400 m, 800 m, 1500 m, mile, 3K, 5K, 10K, half marathon and marathon, at three levels, from a first finish to a performance season.

Does it work with my Garmin?

You can send a planned run or workout to a Garmin watch from the plan. It needs a Garmin watch and a Forge sign-in, and you turn it on in Settings. Forge doesn't record GPS runs: your watch or phone does that.
