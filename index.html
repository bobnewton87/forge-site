Why Forge exists

Why Forge exists

“It's motivating to see a 3D model of yourself looking good. It makes you want to do it.”

I train in different places. If I'm in Taiwan, I might only have a hotel room, but I still want my workout in, the right calories, and to see what I'll look like if I keep doing this workout and eating this food for four weeks or more.

I wanted to plan an ideal workout in any environment, weeks ahead, with the click of a button, and change it when I move. That's Forge. It knows your goals, anybody can use it, and it's fun, because you watch a 3D you get better and better.

The founder of Forge

Meet the founder

Science-first training, built by a 2:30 marathoner

I'm Bob. My first marathon took 3:58. I went deep into the physiology and the research, coached myself with periodized training, and got to 2:30: eight marathons under three hours, the fastest Texan at Boston in 2021, and a spot in the American Development Program at Chicago in 2022. I did it as a dad of three with a sales job that keeps me on the road, so every session had to count.

At 39 I'm putting the same science-first, periodized approach into strength, for health and the long run. Forge is how I train now: hotel gyms, early mornings, whatever time I've got. I'm still a marathoner at heart and I love coaching runners. Now we get to do this part together.

FAQ

Questions

When can I get Forge?

Forge is coming to the App Store for iPhone. You can get early access to the beta now: enter your email and we'll send the link, while seats last. Everyone who signs up is also on the launch list and gets an email when Forge launches.

Do I need a gym?

No. Tell Forge what's around you, from nothing at all to a full gym, and it builds from that: bodyweight moves in a hotel room, dumbbells at home, machines and cables at the gym. Each place keeps its own equipment.

How does Forge plan my workouts?

From your goal, your experience, the muscles you want to focus on, the days and minutes you have, and the equipment where you are. It counts the hard sets each muscle gets each week against a target, suggests more weight once you hit the top of your reps, and plans around what you actually did.

Can I change the plan?

Any time. Swap two days, change where you're training and re-suggest, change a workout's minutes or swap an exercise. Change your usual week and re-plan the weeks ahead in one tap. Nothing is locked in.

How does the 3D projection work?

Forge builds a 3D body from your measurements and projects it forward from your plan, training, protein and calories. It's an estimate based on published research: it shows a likely range, not a promise, and it updates as you log. Your results will differ.

Does it do nutrition, including low-carb and keto?

Yes, if you want it. Nutrition is optional. Turn it on for calorie and protein targets for your goal, and choose how much of your calories come from fat, up to 80% for low-carb and keto.

What does Forge Pro cost?

$9.99 a month or $59.99 a year in the US. The yearly plan starts with a 2-week free trial for new subscribers. Free to download: the free version has your 3D character in a still pose, workouts and runs you log yourself, the exercise library, tape measurements and the weight log, and 3 one-tap workouts a week. Forge Pro adds the projection, your character in motion, the 3D exercise videos, planning ahead, nutrition and sync.

Is my data private?

Your data stays on your phone and Forge works offline. Sync, part of Pro, is end-to-end encrypted: your data is locked on your phone before it's uploaded, so we can't read it. You can export a backup or delete your account any time.

Is Forge medical advice?

No. Forge is a training and wellness tool, not medical advice. If you have a health condition or an injury, or you're pregnant, check with your doctor before you start.
