Forge Privacy Policy

Forge is a workout app made by Artificial General Intelligence LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information Forge handles, where it goes, and what you can do about it.

The short version

  • Forge works without an account. Everything you enter stays on your phone unless you choose to turn on cloud sync.
  • If you turn on cloud sync, your Forge data is stored on our servers so it can reach your other devices. We use it only to run the app for you.
  • We don't show ads, and we don't sell or share your information for advertising. If you are 13 or older we use Google Analytics to count visits and which screens are used (never your workouts or body data), and you can turn it off in Settings. Section 3e has the details. Nothing is collected this way from anyone under 13.
  • You can export your data at any time, and you can delete your cloud account from inside the app.

1. What Forge stores on your phone

Forge keeps your information in the app's private storage on your device. It includes what you choose to enter:

  • Profile: your first name (optional), units, goal, experience level, training preferences, the muscles you want to focus on, and your weekly schedule.
  • Gyms: the gyms or places you train and the equipment at each.
  • Workouts: exercises, sets, reps, weights, cardio, any titles and notes you add, rest-timer settings and personal records.
  • Body: height, birth year, sex, bodyweight entries, tape measurements and body-fat estimates, which Forge also uses to draw your 3D body.
  • Nutrition: calorie and protein targets and any daily entries you log.
  • Health details you add: supplements you take, sleep and stress ratings, aches or pains you ask Forge to work around, and, if you use the female settings, menopause stage and pregnancy status. (A profile restored from a backup made with the web version can hold other fields, such as lab values; the app keeps and syncs them with your profile but never reads them.)

All of this is optional except what a feature needs to work (for example, a workout needs to know which gym you are in). Forge's estimates and projections are calculated on your phone.

This information never leaves your phone unless you turn on cloud sync (section 2), export a backup (section 4), or contact us and include it yourself.

1b. Apple Health (iPhone, only if you connect it)

In the iPhone app you can connect Forge to Apple Health (More → Settings → Apple Health). iOS then asks you which items Forge may read and write, and you can allow some and not others or change your mind at any time in the Health app.

  • What Forge reads, if you allow it: your workouts recorded by other apps and devices (type, start time, duration, active energy and distance, and the name of the app or device that recorded them), your weight, your body-fat percentage, your daily step count and your daily active energy.
  • What Forge does with it: it adds those workouts to your Forge history (once, even if several apps recorded the same workout), puts your weigh-ins and body-fat readings in your body log, and uses your steps to set your daily activity level for your calorie estimates. Active energy is shown to you but not used in calculations.
  • What Forge writes, if you allow it: each workout you finish in Forge, with its time, Forge's calorie estimate and any distance. Forge only ever changes or deletes the workouts it wrote itself.
  • Where it goes: what Forge imports becomes part of your Forge data on your phone (section 1). If you use cloud sync (section 2), it syncs end-to-end encrypted like the rest of your Forge data; we can't read it. Forge doesn't store Apple Health data in iCloud.
  • What we never do with it: we don't use information from Apple Health for advertising, marketing or data mining, we don't sell it, and we don't share it with anyone, including the email service in section 3b.
  • Stopping: More → Settings → Apple Health → Stop syncing. What was already imported stays in Forge until you delete it; to take back Forge's permissions, use the Health app (your profile picture → Apps → Forge).

The home-screen widget and the lock-screen workout show your plan, streak and current exercise on your phone only; they contain no Apple Health information and nothing from them leaves your phone.

2. Cloud sync (Forge Pro)

If you have Forge Pro and create a Forge account, your Forge data is encrypted on your phone, with a key only your devices have, and then copied to our servers so you can use it on more than one device and get it back if you lose your phone. We can't read it. If Pro ends, uploads stop, but you can still download or delete what is stored.

  • What we collect: your email address, an account ID, and the Forge data listed in section 1, end-to-end encrypted. Your password never leaves your phone: Forge turns it into one key that signs you in (our sign-in provider stores only a secure hash of it) and another that locks your data. Neither we nor our hosting provider can read your synced data. We can see your email address, your account ID, when you sign in and sync, how many items are synced (for example one per workout), when each last changed, and its size. A few screen settings (such as the last tab you opened) and any test accounts you create on the device never sync.
  • Recovery code: when you set up sync, Forge shows a recovery code once. We don't keep it. If you forget your password, it's the only way to unlock your synced data. Without it you can start over with the data on one of your devices, and anything that was only in the cloud is lost. We can't recover it for you.
  • Why: only to provide sync and backup for you.
  • Who stores it: our hosting provider, Supabase, Inc., stores the data on our behalf in the United States (Supabase on Amazon Web Services, region us-east-1, Northern Virginia). Supabase encrypts data in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256), on top of Forge's own end-to-end encryption, and each account can read and change only its own rows. 3. Purchases

Forge Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment. We never see your card or bank details. To check whether you have an active subscription, Forge uses RevenueCat, Inc., which receives your App Store purchase receipts and a random app user ID, or your Forge account ID once you are signed in. RevenueCat doesn't receive your workouts, body or health information. Our server keeps your subscription status (product, trial, store and expiry date) to decide whether sync may save your data; it is deleted with your account.

3b. Marketing emails (only if you ask)

You can ask for training tips and Forge news by ticking a box when you set up the app, in Settings, when you create an account, or by joining the waitlist on our website. The box is never ticked for you, and nothing in Forge depends on it.

Early access to the beta. The website's early-access form is the same sign-up. We use your email to send you the link to the Forge beta and how to install it (one message, from our sign-in service, about your access), and, as with the waitlist, to confirm your address and send launch news and the occasional training tip. The beta needs an account, so sign-ups here are for ages 13 and up: we ask for your birth year to check this, and we do not keep it. If you are under 13 nothing is sent or saved. Beta places are limited; if they have run out you are put on the waitlist only.

  • What we keep: your email address, where you signed up, when, the exact wording you agreed to, the app version, whether you confirmed, a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address to stop abuse, and your account ID if you were signed in.
  • Why: to send the emails you asked for. The legal basis is your consent, confirmed by a link in the first email.
  • Who sends them: Kit (ConvertKit LLC) stores your address and sends the emails for us.
  • Stopping: use the unsubscribe link in any email, or Settings → Account → Unsubscribe. Deleting your Forge account doesn't unsubscribe you by itself; unsubscribe too if you want no more emails. We keep a record of your consent and of the unsubscribe as proof, and nothing else.

3c. Invites and offer codes

If you invite friends, we keep your invite code, which account used whose code and when, the App Store offer codes we hand out, whether the friend's first payment went through or was refunded, any free month you earned, and a scrambled (hashed) form of the requester's IP address for up to 2 days to stop abuse. RevenueCat receives the free month so Pro unlocks. If you delete your account, your code is deleted and the invite history is kept without your account ID. Sharing uses your phone's own share sheet, and the rating prompt is Apple's; Forge sees neither what you share nor how you rate.

3d. Garmin and Strava (only if you connect them)

In Settings → Connections you can connect your Garmin Connect or Strava account to your Forge account. Nothing happens unless you do, and you can disconnect at any time, in Forge or in Garmin Connect or Strava.

  • What Forge receives: for each new activity, its type, start time and time zone, duration, distance, average and highest heart rate, calories, the device that recorded it, and (from Strava) its title. Forge doesn't ask for your location, routes or GPS tracks, photos, your profile or your followers.
  • How it gets here: Garmin or Strava tells our server about a new activity, and our server fetches it using the permission you gave. Our server reads it once, in memory, to put it in Forge's format, then encrypts it so that only your devices can open it (with a key made on your device and locked with your end-to-end encryption key) and stores it until one of your devices picks it up. Your device then adds it to your Forge log, which syncs end-to-end encrypted like the rest of your data, and our server deletes its copy. Anything not picked up is deleted after 30 days (Strava: 7 days).
  • Strava: Strava's rules for apps let Forge show you your own Strava activities for up to 7 days, but not keep them. So Forge shows your last 7 days of Strava activities in Settings → Connections and never adds them to your workouts; you log what you want yourself. Strava data is shown only to you, never shared, and never used for advertising or artificial intelligence. Strava may collect and use usage data about how apps use its API, under Strava's own privacy policy.
  • Garmin: activities from Garmin are added to your Forge log automatically, and shown with the Garmin device that recorded them.
  • What Forge sends (only if you turn it on): with Send planned workouts to Garmin, Forge sends your planned workouts for the next 7 days (names, exercises, sets, reps, rest times, weights and target paces) to your Garmin Connect calendar so they reach your watch. Garmin handles them under its own terms and privacy policy and may keep and use them.
  • What our server keeps while you are connected: your Garmin user ID or Strava athlete ID, the permissions you granted, your connection settings, when you connected and when the last activity arrived, and the access keys Garmin or Strava gave us, which are encrypted on our server and never sent to your phone. Unlike your synced data, these keys can be used by our server: that is how activities arrive while the app is closed. For workouts sent to Garmin we keep which Forge workout went to which Garmin workout and date.
  • Disconnecting: Forge tells Garmin or Strava to end the connection and deletes the access keys, anything waiting for your devices and (Garmin) the workouts it put on your Garmin calendar from today on. For Garmin you choose whether workouts already in your Forge log stay or go. Deleting your Forge account deletes all of the above.
  • Why: to log your training and send your plan to your watch, as you asked. Legal basis (EU and UK): your request (contract), and for health information your explicit consent, given when you connect.

Sending a workout to your Garmin watch (only if you turn it on)

With Send to watch (Settings → Garmin; Forge Pro, and Pro+Run for runs) you link the Forge app on your Garmin watch to your Forge account with a code, and then send a planned workout to it with one tap.

  • This is the one thing that is not end-to-end encrypted. Your watch can't unseal what Forge seals, so the workout you send sits on our server in readable form until your watch downloads it.
  • What is sent: only that workout: its name and its steps (exercise names, sets, reps, weights and rests, or a run's steps). Nothing else about you.
  • How long: it is erased as soon as your watch downloads it, or deleted after 24 hours if it never does. At most ten wait at a time.
  • What we keep for a linked watch: a one-way hash of the watch's secret (not the secret itself), the name you gave it, and when it was linked and last seen, until you unlink it, turn Send to watch off or delete your account.
  • Off until you turn it on. Turning it off unlinks your watches and deletes anything still waiting. It is for accounts 13 and older.
  • The Forge watch app is installed from Garmin's Connect IQ Store, which Garmin runs under its own terms and privacy policy.

3e. Usage statistics (Google Analytics)

Forge uses Google Analytics 4, a service of Google LLC, on the website and in the app, so we can see how many people use Forge, which screens they open and for how long. It is for counting use and improving the app, not for advertising.

What is collected:

  • On the website: the pages and buttons used (the waitlist button, the demo videos, the questions opened, the pricing section), how far down the page you scroll, how long you stay, the page you came from, the channel link you used (for example ?via=tiktok), and your approximate location (country or city, from your IP address; Google doesn't keep the full address).
  • In the app: the name of each screen you open, how long the app is open, and a few counts with no values: that setup finished, that a workout was built or logged, and that the Pro offer or paywall was shown or a free trial started. The app is the web app, the beta or the iPhone app, and the data includes your device type, browser or iOS version, language and approximate location.

What is never sent: your workouts, sets, weights, measurements, body fat, photos, food log, health or Apple Health data, medications, labs, notes, gym names, your name, your email address, anything you type, your account or sign-in, or anything from end-to-end encrypted sync. We don't send a user ID to Google. Google Analytics gets a random identifier that is not linked to your Forge account.

Cookies and storage: on the website and the web app, Google Analytics sets two first-party cookies (_ga and _ga_ plus an ID) with a random identifier, which expire after at most two years. In the iPhone app there are no cookies: the app keeps a random identifier on the phone for the same purpose. We don't use Google signals, advertising features or ad personalization, and we turn off Google's advertising data sharing. Google keeps the data for 14 months, then deletes it.

Where consent is required (the EEA, the UK and Switzerland): analytics cookies and identifiers are off by default there and Forge doesn't switch them on, so no analytics cookie is set and no identifier is stored on your device. Google may still receive a basic, cookieless signal that a page or screen was opened, without a device identifier. Your choices:

  • In the app: More → Settings → About → Share anonymous usage stats. It is on by default for adults and teens. When it is off, nothing is sent to Google and the app doesn't load Google's script.
  • On the website: we honour the Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals from your browser: with either on, Google Analytics isn't loaded. You can also block it with a browser extension, or install Google's opt-out add-on (tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
  • Under 13: Forge never loads Google Analytics for anyone it knows is under 13, and doesn't load it in the app until you have given a birth year that shows you are 13 or older. Nothing in Settings is needed for this. The app has no analytics for ages 11 and 12.

Google's use of the data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google Analytics is not advertising: Google doesn't use this data to show you ads, and we don't share it with advertisers.

4. Backups you export

More → Backup lets you download or copy a file containing your Forge data. That file goes wherever you choose (for example Files, iCloud Drive or an email). Once you save or send it, it is handled by that service, not by us.

Each exercise, in the library and in a workout's exercise details, has a link to a YouTube search for that exercise. Tapping it opens Safari or the YouTube app outside Forge with the exercise name as the search, and YouTube's own privacy policy applies. Forge sends YouTube nothing else.

6. What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising SDKs. The only analytics is Google Analytics, as section 3e describes: counts of screens and a few events, never your health or body data, off in Settings and never for under-13s.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites, and no data brokers.
  • We don't sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • We don't use your health or fitness information, including anything from Apple Health, for marketing, advertising or data mining.
  • Forge reads from and writes to Apple Health only if you connect it, and only as section 1b describes. It doesn't use your location, contacts, camera, photos or microphone. Garmin and Strava data is never sold, used for advertising or used to train or run artificial intelligence.

7. How long we keep it

Our full written policy is in Data retention.

  • On your phone: until you delete it. Deleting the Forge app removes all of its data from that phone.
  • Garmin and Strava: an activity waits on our server until one of your devices picks it up, at most 30 days (Strava: 7 days). Connection details and access keys are kept until you disconnect or delete your account.
  • Send to watch: a workout you send is erased when your watch downloads it, or after 24 hours. A linked watch's details are kept until you unlink it, turn Send to watch off or delete your account.
  • Usage statistics: Google Analytics keeps its data for 14 months and then deletes it (section 3e).
  • In the cloud: until you delete it or delete your account. When you delete your cloud account, your login and all of your synced data are removed from our live database immediately. Encrypted backups made by our hosting provider are kept for up to 7 days and then overwritten.

8. Your choices and rights

You can do all of these yourself in the app:

  • See and export your data: More → Backup → Download.
  • Correct it: edit any entry in the app.
  • Delete it from a phone: More → Settings → About → Erase all data on this device, then type ERASE. This removes the active account's workouts, plans, body data, food log, gyms and settings from the phone. Other accounts on the phone and any cloud copy are kept (a signed-in account signs out first, so nothing syncs back). Deleting the Forge app also erases everything on the phone. If the phone has more than one Forge account, you can also remove one at More → Settings → Account → Delete from this device.
  • Delete your cloud account: More → Settings → Account → tap your account → Delete cloud account, then type DELETE. This permanently removes your login and everything synced to it. Forge then asks whether to keep a copy on this phone.
  • Stop syncing: sign out from the same screen. Your data stays on the phone.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, California or other US states), you may also have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Email us at support@forgetrainanywhere.com and we will reply within 30 days. We won't treat you differently for using these rights. If you are in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Legal bases (EU and UK): we process your sync data to provide the service you asked for (contract), health information in the cloud with your explicit consent, and server logs for security (legitimate interests).

9. Children

Forge is for people aged 11 and over. Forge asks for your birth year when you set it up, and if you are under 11 it stops and keeps nothing.

  • Ages 11 to 12: Forge works entirely on the device. There is no account, no cloud sync, no marketing email, no Apple Health connection, no feedback form and no invite or creator codes. We collect nothing from you. See section 9a.
  • Ages 13 to 17: you may use the features above with a parent or guardian's permission (see the Terms). Forge shows you a simpler app: no weight-loss or body-fat goals, no hormone, medication or lab screens, and moderate training. We never send marketing email to anyone under 18.

11. Security

Data on your phone is protected by your device's own security (passcode, Face ID or Touch ID). Synced data is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before it's uploaded, using a key made from your password that never leaves your devices; our servers refuse anything that isn't encrypted. It's also encrypted in transit and at rest, and each account can access only its own rows. A strong password matters, so we ask for at least 12 characters. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your information, we will tell you as the law requires.
