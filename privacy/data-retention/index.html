Artificial General Intelligence LLC keeps personal information only as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected for, and then deletes it. We never keep it indefinitely.

InformationWhy we have itHow long we keep it

Synced data in the cloud (ages 13 and over, encrypted on your device)To sync between your devicesUntil you delete it or your cloud account. Deleting the account removes it from our live database immediately.
